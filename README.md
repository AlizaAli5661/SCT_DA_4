# SCT_DA_4 - Marketing Campaign Business Insights

## 📊 Task 4: Business Insights Report

This project analyzes marketing campaign performance to identify
important business insights related to customer engagement,
conversion rates, revenue, cost efficiency, and ROI.

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective is to evaluate the performance of different
marketing platforms and provide data-driven recommendations for
better campaign optimization.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook
- Excel

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains 763 marketing campaign records with information
about:

- Campaign details
- Marketing platforms
- Target audience
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Leads
- Applications
- Enrollments
- Cost
- Revenue
- Region

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

- Data inspection and cleaning
- Duplicate and missing-value checks
- Platform-wise performance analysis
- Marketing funnel analysis
- CTR calculation
- Conversion rate analysis
- ROI calculation
- Cost per lead analysis
- Cost per enrollment analysis
- Revenue and enrollment comparison
- Business insights and recommendations

## 📈 Visualizations

The project includes 8 visualizations covering:

1. Impressions by Platform
2. Clicks by Platform
3. Leads by Platform
4. Enrollments by Platform
5. Revenue by Platform
6. ROI by Platform
7. Marketing Cost by Platform
8. Marketing Funnel Performance

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify high-performing marketing platforms,
platforms with better ROI and cost efficiency, and areas where the
marketing funnel can be improved.

## 📌 Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-ROI platforms.
- Maintain platforms generating strong revenue and enrollments.
- Review low-performing channels for cost optimization.
- Improve conversion rates at each stage of the marketing funnel.
- Use data-driven platform allocation for future campaigns.
